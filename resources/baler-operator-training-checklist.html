Baler operator training checklist

Train on the specific machine using the manufacturer’s manual and your facility’s written procedures. This checklist records that each topic was covered and demonstrated; it does not replace the manual, a lockout/tagout procedure, or the Department of Labor notice required when workers under 18 load the machine. No one under 18 may operate or unload a baler.
