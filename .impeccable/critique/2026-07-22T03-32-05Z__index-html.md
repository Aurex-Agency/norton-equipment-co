## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: passes the 5-second layperson test (real photos, real names, ownable ph-frame detailing, voice-y copy) but FAILS at second glance for a designer: the section grammar is the saturated AI kit. Systemic eyebrow scaffolding (.eyebrow on every section plus 9 more tracked-caps microlabel selectors), 29 near-identical cards in four consecutive grids on home, numbered markers on non-sequences (services 01-09, benefits 01-04), stats strip = hero-metric template with count-up, and the full 2024-25 motion kit deployed simultaneously (progress bar, reveals, count-up, marquee, Ken Burns, magnetic, tilt, parallax bolts, scan line). Identity-preservation defends the steel/gold/cream palette and Lora accent (logo-derived), but .scan and .ghostnum are ambient-tech decoration with no register reason on an honest/tough/neighborly brand.

Deterministic scan (14 pages): 6 CLI findings, exit 2. numbered-section-markers x5 (home, blog index, quote, self-contained, compactor-repair), monotonous-spacing x1 (olive-branch-ms, mostly an inline-style-duplication artifact: 12 repeated link chips). Runtime injection (3 pages): home 74 findings incl. repeated-section-kickers x8, all-caps-body x10, low-contrast x27 (subset reliable), dark-glow x6, thin-border-wide-shadow x30 (7 on hidden dropdowns).

False positives confirmed by control test: gradient-text and theater-slop-phrase fire on every page (detector matches its own injected source; neither pattern exists in repo). Low-contrast entries reporting #ffffff backgrounds under dark gradient sections are misresolved; the gold-on-white ones (#a97c1c at 3.3-3.8:1) are genuine and match Assessment A's manual math.

Visual overlays: not available (headless environment, no human-visible browser); programmatic injection was used instead and its console output is summarized above.

## Overall Impression

This is a high-craft site with a real voice and real proof that undermines itself twice: the two most valuable user actions (calling and submitting the form) are the two least-designed paths, and the home page buries its genuinely distinctive assets (finder, shop-floor photos, Williams-Sonoma testimonials) under 6,000px of identical card catalog. Fix the two P0s and restructure home, and this moves from "polished template" to "brand staple."

## What's Working

1. The .ph-frame photo treatment (notched gold corner + hazard base): ownable, logo-derived, consistently applied; no template ships it.
2. The machine finder: the counter conversation as a widget; honest options, 2-4 choices per step, noscript fallback. The single most on-brand artifact.
3. Micro-copy at decision moments: "Sold by some. Serviced by Norton.", "Talk to a real tech, not a call center", "Sizing from reality beats sizing from a form."

## Priority Issues

- [P0] Interior hero gutters are zero on every interior page. .phero-in's padding shorthand (site.css) overrides .wrap's horizontal padding: text flush to screen edge at 390px, 48px misaligned at 1440px, on ~90 generated pages including every local-SEO city page. Fix: restore horizontal padding in the .phero-in rule (use padding-block or add the clamp inline padding). Suggested: /impeccable polish
- [P0] Quote form is a mailto trap on the actual Vercel deployment. The Netlify gate (data-host attribute) is never set by any page, so every submit opens an email draft or does nothing visibly; no success/error state exists in CSS. Fix: real endpoint (Vercel function or form service) + designed success and inline error states + phone in the failure path. Suggested: /impeccable harden
- [P1] Phone vanishes from the sticky header at 960-1500px, on a site whose primary CTA is the call and whose personas arrive mid-problem. Fix: icon-only .nav-call at all widths; consider making Call the gold button and Quote the ghost. Suggested: /impeccable polish
- [P1] Home page is four consecutive identical card grids (9+7+9+4). The biggest AI tell and the worst cognitive load: the buyer decision is binary (wet/dry) but faces 9 equal cards; the finder that solves it sits below them. Fix: restructure per section (finder promoted above/into the compactors section as a 2-branch decision, services as compact featured-3 + checklist, brands as marquee + links), and thin the eyebrow/numbering scaffolding while in there. Suggested: /impeccable layout (structure) + /impeccable quieter (scaffolding/ornament)
- [P2] WCAG AA contrast failures on gold-deep text: #a97c1c on white/paper = 3.3-3.8:1 for prose links, card "go" links, tl-year, paper eyebrows; placeholders unstyled UA gray. Confirmed by both assessments independently. Fix: darken text-role gold to ~#8a6414, style placeholders. Suggested: /impeccable audit (verify) then /impeccable polish

## Minor Observations

- .scan laser line + .ghostnum outline numerals: ambient-tech decoration, register mismatch, cut candidates.
- Testimonials (the best proof on the site) get equal-card treatment; one full-width featured Williams-Sonoma quote would outperform three cards.
- Interest select defaults to "Trash compactor - purchase" with no "Choose..." option: silent wrong data from service customers.
- Marquee has no pause mechanism (WCAG 2.2.2).
- Blog cards on home lack the images that exist on blog index cards.
- Home hero clamp max 100px exceeds the 96px display ceiling.
- Mobile bar can visually collide with last content tile on short pages.
- Protect: noscript finder fallback, print styles, safe-area-inset, draft-note component.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the phone call is the business, why is the phone always the ghost button? What does the site look like with Call as the gold action everywhere and Quote as the fallback?

3. Dale's compactor is down at 2 PM Tuesday: where is his front door? Does Norton need a third hero path ("Machine down? Techs on the road today") more than it needs the Balers card?
