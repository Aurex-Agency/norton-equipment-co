Compactor Repair & Service

Repair and emergency service for every compactor make and model.

Any Brand · Any ModelOur Own TechsTN · MS · AR
Request a Quote → Call (662) 838-7900

A down compactor backs up a whole operation fast: waste piles on the dock, the hauler cannot pull a jammed container, and suddenly the least glamorous machine on the property is the most urgent. Compactor repair is one of the most common calls we run — and one we have been answering across the Mid-South since 1997.

Our techs troubleshoot and repair every major brand: Marathon, Cram-A-Lot, Max-Pak, PTR, Wastequip, and the rest. Hydraulic cylinders and power units, valves and hoses, limit switches and photo eyes, control panels, door latches and hinges, rams and ram shoes — and when the problem is structural, our in-house welding and fabrication shop rebuilds floors, hooks, and guides that other service companies can only quote replacements for.

Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get to a down compactor?
We prioritize down-machine calls and dispatch across our 100-mile service radius from Byhalia — typically same-day or next-day depending on location and parts. Call (662) 838-7900 and we will give you a real ETA, not a window.
My compactor is from a hauler — can you work on it?
If the hauler owns the machine, service is contractually theirs. If you own or rent it from anyone else, we can work on it. Not sure? We can help you read the contract — and show you what owning your own equipment saves.
Do you stock compactor parts?
We stock common wear and electrical parts and have overnight relationships for the rest. Our fab shop covers the structural side same-week in most cases.
