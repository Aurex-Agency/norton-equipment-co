Equipment Logistics

Waste and recycling equipment delivered, installed, and commissioned.

Any Brand · Any ModelOur Own TechsTN · MS · AR
Call (662) 838-7900 Request a Quote →

A ten-thousand-pound baler does not deliver itself. Getting waste and recycling equipment onto a site, through the building, onto the pad, powered, tested, and running (and getting the old machine out) is its own trade, and it is one of ours.

We deliver and install the equipment we sell, but the service stands on its own too: relocating machines between facilities, extracting dead equipment from tight back rooms, and hauling away machines we buy from closing operations. Our fabrication shop builds the odd bracket, chute, or dock plate that every real-world install eventually needs.

This page is about waste and recycling equipment specifically. If you need machinery of any other kind moved, set, or torn out, that is our rigging service.

What’s Included

How we handle it.

Delivery & placement

Trucking, offloading, and precision placement of balers and compactors, including tight-clearance back-room sets.

Turnkey installation

Anchoring, power coordination, chute and feed hookups, commissioning, and operator training in one scheduled visit.

Machine relocations

Teardown, transport, reinstallation, and recommissioning when a baler or compactor moves between buildings, with downtime planned in hours, not weeks.

Removal & buyback

Dead machine in the way? We extract and haul it, and if it has value, we will buy it and put that toward its replacement.

Down machine? We prioritize those calls.

Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you remove a baler from inside a store?
Yes: extracting vertical balers through retail back rooms and narrow corridors is routine work for our crews, done outside business hours when needed.
Do you move equipment you didn’t sell?
All the time. We relocate and install machines from any source, and we will inspect them during the move so you know what condition they arrived in.
Do you move machinery that isn’t recycling equipment?
Yes, that is our rigging service. Same crews and same forklifts, but the machine does not have to be a baler or a compactor.
How far will you deliver?
Standard logistics coverage matches our 100-mile service radius from the Memphis area; longer hauls are quoted case by case. Ask.
