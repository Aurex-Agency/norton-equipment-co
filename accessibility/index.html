The Fine Print

Accessibility Statement

Norton Equipment Co. is committed to making its website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every visitor to be able to find information about our equipment and services and to reach us easily.

Our Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which are the standards referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, hearing, cognitive, and motor impairments.

What We Have Done

Steps we have taken on this website include:

  • Semantic, structured HTML with a “Skip to content” link and clear heading order.
  • Text and interface colors chosen to meet AA contrast ratios.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Labeled forms with clear instructions and status messages.
  • Full keyboard operability, with a visible focus indicator for keyboard users.
  • Support for the operating system's “reduce motion” setting, which pauses non-essential animation.
  • Responsive layouts that work across phones, tablets, and desktops.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort rather than a one-time task. We review new content and features as they are added and work to correct issues we become aware of. Some content provided by third parties may not be fully under our control.

Need Help or Found a Problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or if you would like information in a different format, please tell us. We will do our best to help and to fix the issue.
