Vertical Balers

The standard downstroke baler for retail, grocery, and warehouse back rooms.

The vertical downstroke baler is the most common recycling machine in America for a reason: it fits through a standard doorway footprint, runs on modest power, and turns a mountain of loose cardboard into dense, stackable, sellable bales with one operator and a few minutes a day.

We sell and service the full range (from 42-inch balers for small retail up to wide-body 72-inch machines producing mill-size bales) and we stock the wire, do the operator training, and answer the service calls afterward. If a back room in the Mid-South has a baler in it, odds are decent we have had a hand on it.

Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What size vertical baler do I need?
It depends on your weekly cardboard volume and whether you want to sell bales. A store producing 20+ loose yards weekly usually justifies a 60″ machine; serious volume or resale ambitions point to a 72″ high-density. We size it free, on site.
What power does a vertical baler need?
Many run on 208/230V single-phase, normal commercial power. Larger high-density units may want three-phase. We confirm before you order.
How long does installation take?
Typically a half day: set, anchor, wire, test, and train. We handle delivery and rigging with our own equipment logistics crew.
