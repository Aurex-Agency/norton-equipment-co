Auger Compactors

Screw-driven compaction for high-volume cardboard and wood waste.

SalesInstallationService · Any BrandMid-South Delivery
Call (662) 838-7900 Request a Quote →

An auger compactor replaces the ram with a large rotating screw that tears material apart and forces it into the container. It cycles like any compactor, but it is built for a steady, high-volume feed and packs cardboard and wood waste to densities a ram machine struggles to match.

Auger units are at their best on cardboard and wood: high-volume distribution centers, manufacturing lines with continuous cardboard and wood scrap, printers, and packaging plants. They are not the right fit for mixed municipal trash or a general big-box retail waste stream, and we will tell you when a stationary ram unit is the smarter buy.

Why It Works

What you get with Norton.

Built for continuous feed

The screw runs as material arrives, so a cart tipper or conveyor line can keep it fed through a busy shift.

Highest container density

The tearing-and-packing action routinely loads noticeably more weight per container than a comparable ram compactor on cardboard and wood. Fewer pulls, period.

Best on cardboard & wood

Augers earn their keep on cardboard and wood waste. For wet or mixed municipal waste, a self-contained or stationary ram unit is the better tool.

Low jam rates on bulky OCC

The screw self-clears most bridging that stalls ram machines on high-volume corrugated.

Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Auger vs. ram compactor, which is cheaper to run?
On high-volume cardboard and wood, augers pull ahead on hauling savings because container payloads are so much heavier. On lower volumes or mixed waste, a well-sized stationary ram unit is usually the better buy. We will run your numbers honestly.
Is an auger a shredder?
No. An auger does not shred; its screw tears material and forces it into the container. It reduces and densifies, but it is a compactor, not a size-reduction shredder.
Do you service auger compactors?
Yes: screws, drives, gearboxes, and controls, all brands. Our shop also rebuilds flighting and hoppers that other shops would replace outright.
